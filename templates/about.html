About MKW Combo Stats

MKW Combo Stats helps you explore Mario Kart World character and vehicle combos, compare their stats side by side, and find the strongest pick for any map.

What you can do here

  • Home -- pick a character and vehicle to see the combo's full stat breakdown.
  • Compare -- line up multiple combos side by side and instantly spot the best and worst stats.
  • Selection -- choose a map and get the best character/vehicle combo for it, prioritized by speed or turbo.

Where the numbers come from

All stats are sourced from the Mario Kart Wiki community and the game's own files. See the Credits page for details.

Open source
